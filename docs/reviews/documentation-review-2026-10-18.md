# Documentation review: 18 October 2026

## Outcome and scope

Created a [functional inventory](../documentation-inventory.md) of 277 documentation files across 14 areas, plus nine in-app documentation source files. The inventory includes local/ignored documents and labels their status. It excludes dependencies, generated reports, caches, and local agent configuration.

Completed a factual and editorial pass over the 54 maintained references in the documentation validator. Three Luna agents reviewed 44 references and workspace Help; the coordinating agent reviewed ten references, integrated the corrections, checked the in-app documentation sources, and ran final validation. The per-document ledger below records sources and limits. This was a source-based review, not an exhaustive execution of every documented branch.

Historical plans, 143 local progress notes, dated compliance/accessibility/release records, and earlier engineering reviews were inventoried rather than rewritten or revalidated against today's runtime. Their age does not make them current behavior contracts. Licence terms and third-party attribution text were preserved. Operator-published legal content in deployment databases was not inspected.

The user confirmed during this review that the MHRA registration, named Clinical Safety Officer, and approved DCB0129 safety-case claims in `SAFETY.md` are complete, with controlled evidence held outside this repository. Those claims remain unchanged. This review records that confirmation; it does not claim an independent audit of the evidence.

## Writing rules applied

`AGENTS.md` requires concise, concrete, plain English, a preference for active voice, and removal of unnecessary words without losing technical, clinical, security, or legal precision. It also requires current documentation to match implemented behavior, tests, and configuration and preserves dated evidence as a record of what was observed.

The review used the stop-slop skill to identify filler and formulaic phrasing. Project precision takes priority over mechanical style bans. Procedures use visible control names and include the action that completes the workflow. Technical references retain conditions, limits, identifiers, and failure behavior. The [inventory](../documentation-inventory.md#writing-and-review-rules) contains the reusable review sequence.
